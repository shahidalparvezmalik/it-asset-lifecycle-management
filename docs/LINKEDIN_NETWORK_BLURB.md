IT Asset Management is more than maintaining an inventory of devices. Its value comes from turning lifecycle data into decisions that strengthen operational visibility, warranty monitoring, risk review, and refresh planning.

In my latest article, I explore how data-quality checks, explainable risk scoring, and lifecycle indicators can help IT Operations teams identify records that may need attention and make better-informed decisions. Transparency, governance, and human oversight matter whenever automation is introduced into asset management.

The [IT Asset Lifecycle Management proof of concept](https://github.com/shahidalparvezmalik/it-asset-lifecycle-management) uses fictional asset records and transparent rules to highlight data-quality issues, support and warranty exposure, and refresh-review priorities. It is read-only: it does not connect to production inventory systems, make purchasing decisions, or retire or alter assets.

For IT Operations and Service Delivery leaders: **Which lifecycle signal is most often overlooked in your environment—support status, warranty, ownership, data quality, or refresh planning?**

# ITAM Operating Model

## Purpose

Define a lightweight governance model for the demonstration. Adapt ownership and control points to the organization's approved policies before production use.

## Roles

| Role | Example accountability |
|---|---|
| Asset owner | Business purpose, assignment accuracy, review of exceptions |
| IT asset manager | Register quality, lifecycle policy, reconciliation and reporting |
| Service owner | Service criticality, dependency context and impact assessment |
| Procurement / Finance | Purchase evidence, supplier terms, cost and budget approvals |
| Security / Data Protection | Security requirements, data sanitization and disposal controls |
| Change / Service Management | Coordinating material changes and service-impacting refreshes |

## Minimum control points

1. Create the asset record from an authoritative receipt or approved inventory source.
2. Validate unique asset identity and required ownership fields.
3. Record assignment, location and lifecycle status changes promptly.
4. Review warranty, support and planned refresh milestones on a defined cadence.
5. Reconcile records against approved inventory sources and investigate exceptions.
6. Require approval for procurement, reassignment where policy requires, and retirement.
7. Verify data sanitization and disposal evidence before closing retirement.
8. Retain an audit trail of changes and the identity of the approver.

## Refresh prioritization

The sample scoring model is intentionally transparent and illustrative. It combines support status, warranty, planned refresh date, condition, criticality and missing ownership. It is not statistically calibrated and does not predict failures. Production policy should consider service dependencies, vendor lifecycle notices, security posture, repair economics, lead times and budget constraints.

## Suggested review cadence

- Critical infrastructure: risk-based and aligned with service review cadence.
- Warranty and vendor support: monthly review of near-term expirations.
- General workplace devices: quarterly lifecycle review.
- Register reconciliation: frequency based on data volatility and control requirements.
- Retirement evidence: each retirement event, before record closure.

## Governance warning

A high score is a prompt for review, not an automatic purchase or disposal instruction. Validate the data and service impact, document the decision, and follow delegated approval authority.
